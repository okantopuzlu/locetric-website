Most independent hotels still price by instinct.

Without a revenue management team, rates follow habit, occupancy hunches or whatever a competitor shows that day. The data exists. What is missing is someone with the analytical capacity to turn it into decisions every day.

  • Demand rises while room rates stay flat for weeks.
  • The hotel sells out long before its competitors, without raising its price.
  • Rates on the official website and on booking sites drift apart.
  • Weekdays, weekends and events are all priced the same.
  • Management cannot benchmark the hotel objectively against its market.

For a local revenue manager, every one of these hotels is a potential client. LOCETRIC finds them and gives you the tools to serve many of them well.

From market data to pricing decisions in three steps.

Each layer feeds the next. The competitors and market signals Scout tracks carry straight into RM Manager when a hotel becomes your client.

1

Market intelligence

Collects hotels, competitors, rates, channels and availability every day, and sets aside prices that are not comparable before any scoring.

2

LOCETRIC Scout

Reviews every hotel in a region against seven criteria and scores revenue opportunity, evidence confidence and sales priority separately.

3

LOCETRIC RM Manager

Supports your clients’ daily pricing and channel decisions, tracks whether each one was applied, and measures its result.

Scout weighs opportunity across seven criteria.

Prices and availability are observed daily over a 30-day window. Each criterion’s weight reflects its impact on revenue.

25 20 15 15 10 10 5
  • Demand response25 pts

    How the hotel’s price reacts when demand and competitor rates move.

  • Early sell-out20 pts

    Whether the hotel sells out earlier than competitors, especially at a relatively low price.

  • Competitive positioning15 pts

    Price position against comparable competitors and the hotel’s own product.

  • Pricing inertia15 pts

    Rates left unchanged longer than market conditions justify.

  • Direct booking leakage10 pts

    Gaps between official-site and booking-site rates, and lost direct bookings.

  • Weekly demand strategy10 pts

    Rate differences by weekday, weekend, events and booking window.

  • Pricing anomalies5 pts

    Unexplained rate jumps and availability inconsistencies.
